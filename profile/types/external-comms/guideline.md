# External communications

- Start from an approved brief. Do not draft directly from a transcript.
- Lead with the change in the reader's world, not the product name.
- Make the reason for saying this now visible near the top.
- Trace every factual claim to the brief's source list.
- Carry the brief's `Not saying` section into the draft as a hard boundary.
- Use direct, confident language for a real launch. Do not add jokes just to sound like Lemonade.
- An attributed quote must be supported by the transcript and sound like the named person.
- External communications always receive human review.
