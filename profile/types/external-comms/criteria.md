# External communications criteria

Score each applicable question 0, 1 or 2. A missing source for a central claim also fails the
compliance veto. Quote fidelity is N/A when the approved brief deliberately contains no quote.

The three criteria marked `audit: false` check a draft against its approved brief. They do not
apply when auditing content that never had a brief, such as an already published post.
