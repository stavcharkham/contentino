# Founder essays

Long-form thought leadership in the founder's voice: strategy, competition, AI, the industry.
Calibrated 2026-08-22 from full lemonade.com essays, which the original research could not
fetch.

- Carry the argument with a concrete analogy or story; the analogy does the work, it does not
  decorate.
- Name the adversary and quote them. Hostile claims trace to named sources, quotes or public
  numbers, linked where possible.
- State numbers as specifics, never rounded into vagueness.
- Joke about strategy, competitors and the industry freely. Never about a customer's claim,
  price or eligibility.
- Close with candour: an explicit admission of uncertainty or limits, not a triumphant bow.
- Em dashes and long sentences are house style here, unlike every other content type.
- First person plural. Never auto-publishes; the named author owns every word.
