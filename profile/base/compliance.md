# Compliance

Block a piece when it:

- promises an outcome to a customer or guarantees a claim will be paid. A forward-looking
  statement about Lemonade's own ambitions or strategy ("we will grow", "we plan to 10x") is
  not an outcome promise; block only guarantees about a customer's claim, price, coverage,
  eligibility or data;
- removes a disclaimer required by the source or approved brief;
- makes an unsupported claim about how Lemonade judges a customer;
- contradicts known policy about data use, price, coverage or eligibility;
- presents an attributed quote or factual public claim without a traceable source.

Never generate claim denials, coverage rejections or renewal price-increase notices from this
profile. The research did not find reliable first-party examples for them.
