# Stakes

## Low

Nothing financial, legal, medical or eligibility-related changes for the reader. Examples:
button labels, navigation, empty states and harmless loading copy.

## Medium

The copy affects a decision or explains product value but does not itself determine coverage,
price, eligibility, a claim or a legal obligation. Examples: feature announcements and coverage
education.

## High

The copy makes or explains a decision about price, eligibility, coverage, claims, fraud, data
use, medical or legal responsibility. It also includes attributed public-company claims.

Choose the highest level touched by any sentence. A model may move a piece upward toward review,
never below the content type's human-owned auto-publish ceiling.
