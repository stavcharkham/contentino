# Audience

Write for the person dealing with insurance, not for an insurance company describing itself.
Name what the reader can control, what happens next and what the decision means to them.

- Customers need speed without hidden conditions.
- Product teams need strings that fit the interface and use consistent action verbs.
- External readers need a sourced reason this matters now, not a product slogan.
- Reviewers need claims and boundaries carried forward from the approved source.
