> Produced for `research/briefs/03-rubric-validation.md`. 36 real Lemonade excerpts (from
> `research/lemonade-corpus.md`) plus 12 deliberately off-brand rewrites of 12 of those same
> messages, shuffled together with no label indicating which is which. Scores are in
> `eval/scores.md`. The mapping between real ids and their off-brand twins is also only in
> `eval/scores.md`, not here.

## Excluded from the corpus, and why

18 of the 54 corpus excerpts were left out:

- **Second-hand or paraphrased, not Lemonade's own words:** LEM-006, LEM-007, LEM-032,
  LEM-033, LEM-034, LEM-035, LEM-039, LEM-041, LEM-047, LEM-048, LEM-050, LEM-051, LEM-052
- **Unconfirmed provenance flagged by the corpus itself** (search-index summary, not a
  direct fetch or confirmed quote): LEM-054
- **Fragments too short to carry a voice judgment** (single button labels, a bare header):
  LEM-005, LEM-011, LEM-014, LEM-016

36 real items remain, which is within the 35-40 target.
