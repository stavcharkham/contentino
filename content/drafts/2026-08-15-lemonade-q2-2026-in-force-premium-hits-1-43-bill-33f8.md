# Lemonade Q2 2026: in-force premium hits $1.43 billion as revenue grows 79%

Lemonade reported its second quarter 2026 results on 7 August. In-force premium reached $1.43 billion, up 32.5% year over year — the eleventh consecutive quarter in which growth accelerated.

Revenue for the quarter was $294 million, an increase of 79%.

The gross loss ratio improved to 60%, from 67%.

Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed by 54%, to $19 million. Lemonade is not yet profitable: this quarter still carried a $19 million adjusted EBITDA loss.

Management reiterated full-year guidance and projected positive adjusted EBITDA in the fourth quarter of 2026. That figure is a projection of where the business is expected to land, not a commitment.

Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.
