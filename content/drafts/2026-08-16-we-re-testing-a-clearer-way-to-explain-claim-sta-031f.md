# We're testing a clearer way to explain claim status

Starting 1 September 2026, we're inviting 200 renters-insurance customers in New York to try a simpler claims-status view for four weeks.

Here's what the test does: it changes how we explain your existing claim status and your next step. That's it. It doesn't change your coverage, how claim decisions get made, when payments arrive, or who's eligible for what. The same claim, the same decision, the same timing, explained more clearly.

We're not trying to make a claim look simpler than it is. Some claims are complicated, and pretending otherwise wouldn't help anyone. What we want is for the next step to be obvious: what's happening now, and what happens next.

## Why now

The test window opens 1 September 2026 and runs four weeks, so we're publishing this before invited customers see an unfamiliar view in their account. It's also worth being clear for everyone else: if you weren't invited, nothing about your claims process has changed. This is a test with 200 customers in New York, not a product change.

## What changed

Only the explanation layer changed. The claims-status view now describes existing claim status and next step in simpler language. Coverage, claim decisions, payment timing and eligibility are unchanged by this test.

## What we're measuring

During the four weeks, we're measuring two things: whether invited customers can identify their next step without contacting support, and whether the new wording reduces repeat questions about status. We don't have results yet. That's the point of running a test first.

## From the team

> The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is.
>
> — Meeting notes, claims-status view test brief

## What this isn't

This test doesn't speed up claim decisions or payments. It doesn't affect the outcome of a claim, only how status and next step are explained. Reducing repeat questions is something we're measuring, not something we're promising. It's not a new claims experience, just a simpler claims-status view, and it's not a general launch: invitation-only, 200 customers, New York, renters insurance only.

We'll share what we learn once the test wraps up.
