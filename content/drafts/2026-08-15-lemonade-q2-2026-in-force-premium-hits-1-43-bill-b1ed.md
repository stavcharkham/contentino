# Lemonade Q2 2026: in-force premium hits $1.43 billion as revenue grows 79%

If you follow Lemonade to see whether growth and discipline can move together, Q2 2026 is the clearest read we've given you yet. We reported these results on the earnings call on 7 August 2026. Here is what they say.

**Growth kept speeding up.** In-force premium reached $1.43 billion, up 32.5% year over year. In-force premium is the annualised value of all the policies our customers currently hold. This was the eleventh straight quarter in which growth accelerated rather than levelled off.
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*

**Revenue grew 79%, to $294 million.**
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*

**Less of each premium dollar went out as claims cost.** The gross loss ratio was 60%, compared with 67%. That ratio is the share of premium paid out on claims.
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*

**The loss got smaller.** Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed 54%, to $19 million. To be plain: Lemonade is not profitable. This quarter carried a $19 million adjusted EBITDA loss, and a smaller loss is still a loss.
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*

**What management said about the rest of the year.** Full-year guidance was reiterated. Management projected positive adjusted EBITDA in Q4 2026. Read that as a projection of where the business is expected to land. It is not a promise, and not a commitment.
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*
