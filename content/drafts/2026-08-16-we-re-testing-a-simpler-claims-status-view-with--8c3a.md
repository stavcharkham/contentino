# We're testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited customers

Starting 1 September 2026, we're testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. The test runs for four weeks.

What's changing is only the way an existing claim's status and next step are explained. It does not change coverage, claim decisions, payment timing or who is eligible. The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make a claim look simpler than it is.

We're measuring two things: whether invited customers can identify their next step without contacting support, and whether the new wording reduces repeat questions about status. A reduction is a goal, not a promised outcome.

This is an invitation-only test, not a general launch, and we haven't decided whether it will become a permanent feature. The test will tell us what customers understand. It won't prove the idea before the results exist.

## Why now

The test window is fixed and narrow: it opens 1 September 2026 and closes four weeks later. We're publishing this now, while the framing is still accurate and before any results exist that would change what we can say. Legal set the boundaries in the 12 August 2026 claims product meeting, so the approved language is available now.

## What changed

The claims product team confirmed the scope, dates and measures for the test, and Legal ruled on how it can be described outside the team. The phrase "new claims experience" was rejected as broader than what's actually changing. The approved description is "a simpler claims-status view." Before this meeting, there was no agreed external framing.

## In Legal's words

> Call it a simpler claims-status view. "New claims experience" is broader than what is changing.
>
> — Priya Shah, Legal

## What this isn't

- This doesn't speed up claim decisions or payments. Legal ruled that out explicitly.
- This doesn't affect the outcome of a claim.
- We're not promising fewer support contacts. A reduction in repeat questions is a goal, not a promised outcome.
- This isn't a new claims experience, a launch, or a feature that's here to stay. It's an invitation-only test, and no decision has been made about making it permanent.
- We don't yet know what customers understood or preferred. Those results don't exist yet.
- Eligibility, coverage and payment timing haven't changed.
