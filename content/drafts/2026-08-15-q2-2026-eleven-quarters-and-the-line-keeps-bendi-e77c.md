# Q2 2026: eleven quarters, and the line keeps bending

Our second quarter numbers are in. The through-line is the same one we have been watching for nearly three years. The book is getting bigger, and it is getting better at the same time.

In-force premium reached $1.43 billion, up 32.5% year over year. That is the eleventh consecutive quarter in which our growth rate has accelerated. Revenue grew 79% to $294 million.

Growth on its own is the easy half. The harder half is what happens to the underwriting as the book expands. This quarter our gross loss ratio improved to 60%, from 67% a year ago.

Those two things together moved the bottom line. Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed 54%, to $19 million. To be clear about what that means: it is a loss. We are not profitable. It is a smaller loss than it was, which is the direction we said we would move in.

Looking ahead, we are reiterating our full-year guidance. We are also projecting positive adjusted EBITDA in Q4 2026. That is a projection based on what we see today, not a promise. Quarters have a way of holding surprises, and we would rather tell you what we expect than tell you what you would like to hear.
