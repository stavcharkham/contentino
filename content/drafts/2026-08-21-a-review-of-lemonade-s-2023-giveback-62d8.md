# A Review of Lemonade's 2023 Giveback

## Our community helped donate over $2 million to 58 charities.

Lemonade's insurance customers select charities during policy signup, and the company donates leftover premium funds to these organizations. In 2023, this initiative generated substantial philanthropic impact across multiple causes.

## Donation Breakdown by Category

**Our Planet and Our Animals: $429,588**

Environmental and animal welfare organizations received nearly $430,000. "Charity: water: $109,941" directed resources toward clean water access in Bangladesh, while "350.org: $94,689" supported anti-fossil fuel campaigns. Tree-planting initiatives through American Forests and Earthday.org contributed over 60,000 trees across multiple regions.

**Global Poverty and Education: $301,842**

This category supported housing, meal assistance, and educational programs. New Story provided vulnerable families with housing in El Salvador, while Citymeals On Wheels delivered over 6,500 meals to elderly New Yorkers.

**Protecting Civil Rights: $582,642**

The largest allocation addressed humanitarian crises and rights protection. "UNICEF USA: $223,621" responded to emergencies in Sudan, and Direct Relief provided disaster assistance in Ukraine with kits for 7,000 people.

**Health: $428,848**

Mental health and disease prevention received significant funding. "To Write Love On Her Arms: $107,416" sponsored therapy sessions, while the National Breast Cancer Foundation received support for mammogram administration.

**LGBTQIA+: $265,927**

The Trevor Project and Ali Forney Center received the largest portions, supporting crisis counseling and youth services for LGBTQ+ individuals.

**Grand Total: $2,008,847 across 58 nonprofits worldwide**
