# Testing a simpler way to explain claim status, with 200 invited customers in New York

Starting 1 September 2026, we're running a four-week test with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. We're trying out clearer wording for one specific thing: how we explain the current status of a claim and the next step to take.

Nothing else changes. Coverage stays the same. Claim decisions stay the same. Payment timing stays the same. Eligibility stays the same. The only thing different for these 200 customers is the wording they see about where their claim stands and what happens next.

This is an invitation-only test, not a launch or a rollout. It is not a new claims experience, it's a simpler claims-status view. We haven't decided whether it will become a permanent feature. That decision depends on what we learn.

## What we're measuring

We're watching two things during the test. First, whether invited customers can identify their next step without needing to contact support. Second, whether the new wording reduces repeat questions about status.

A reduction in repeat questions is a goal we're measuring, not a result we're promising. We're not saying this speeds up decisions or payments. We're not saying it affects how a claim is decided. We're testing whether clearer language helps people understand where things stand, and that's what the test will tell us. It won't prove the idea before the results exist.

## Why now

The test runs from 1 September through the end of September 2026. We're publishing this now so invited customers, and anyone curious about it, can understand what the test is while it's actually running.

## In their own words

Maya Chen, Claims Product Lead, described the goal this way in an internal claims product meeting on 12 August 2026:

> "If we write about it, the useful story is that we are testing clearer explanations of status and next steps with a small invited group. The test will tell us what customers understand. It will not prove the idea before the results exist."

## What this means if you're one of the 200

If you're an invited customer, you'll see the new status wording on an active claim during September. Everything about how your claim is handled, paid and decided stays exactly the same. If you have questions about the test itself, our support team can help.

We'll share what we learn once the test concludes and the results are in.
