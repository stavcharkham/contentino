# Lemonade Q2 2026: in-force premium hits $1.43 billion as revenue grows 79%

If you follow Lemonade to see whether growth and discipline can move in the same direction at the same time, the second quarter of 2026 is the clearest read we have given you yet. We reported these results on the earnings call on 7 August 2026, and here is what they say.

**Growth kept speeding up.** In-force premium — the annualised value of all the policies our customers currently hold — reached $1.43 billion, up 32.5% year over year. That is the eleventh straight quarter in which the rate of growth increased rather than levelled off.
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*

**Revenue grew 79%, to $294 million.**
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*

**Less of each premium dollar went out as claims cost.** The gross loss ratio — the share of premium paid out on claims — was 60%, compared with 67%.
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*

**The loss got smaller.** Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed 54%, to $19 million. To be plain about what that means: Lemonade is not profitable. This quarter carried a $19 million adjusted EBITDA loss, and a smaller loss is still a loss.
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*

**What management said about the rest of the year.** Full-year guidance was reiterated, and management projected positive adjusted EBITDA in the fourth quarter of 2026. Read that as a projection of where the business is expected to land — not a promise, and not a commitment.
*(Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call, 7 August 2026.)*
