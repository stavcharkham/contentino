# Starting 1 September: a small test of clearer claims-status wording

Starting 1 September, we're running a four-week test of clearer claims-status wording with 200 invited customers in New York. The window and the group are already set: this is invitation-only, and nobody outside those 200 customers is part of it.

Here's what's actually changing: the explanations customers see when checking on a claim. That's it. Coverage, claim decisions, payments and eligibility don't change for anyone, inside the test or outside it. The wording a participant sees during the test carries no different meaning for their claim than the wording it replaces.

What we're trying to learn is narrow and specific: can people tell what the next step is without needing to contact support? That's the whole question. We're not measuring speed, and we're not measuring whether claims get easier to resolve. We're measuring whether the words on the screen make the next step clear.

As Maya put it in the product meeting where this test took shape: "The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is."

A few things worth saying plainly. This is a test, not a launch, and we're not announcing general availability. We're not promising an outcome, a result or a wider rollout, and we won't be reporting results here because the test hasn't run yet. Participation is limited to the 200 invited New York customers: there's no way to opt in beyond that group right now.

We'll have more to share once the four weeks are up.
