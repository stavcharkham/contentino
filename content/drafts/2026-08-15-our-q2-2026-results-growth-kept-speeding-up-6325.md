# Our Q2 2026 results: growth kept speeding up

We reported our second-quarter 2026 results on 7 August, and the headline is a number that
has now moved in the same direction eleven quarters in a row.

In-force premium reached $1.43 billion, up 32.5% from a year ago. That makes Q2 the eleventh
consecutive quarter in which our growth accelerated rather than settled. Revenue grew 79% to
$294 million.

The economics underneath that growth improved too. Our gross loss ratio came in at 60%, down
from 67% a year earlier. Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed by 54%, to $19 million. We are still
running a loss, and we are being precise about that: $19 million is smaller than it was, not
gone.

Looking ahead, we reiterated our full-year guidance and projected positive adjusted EBITDA in
the fourth quarter of 2026. That is a projection about where we expect to land, not a promise
we can hand you today.

None of this changes anything about your policy, your price or what you're covered for. It is
a report on how the company is doing, and this quarter it is doing better.

Source: Lemonade Q2 2026 earnings call transcript, The Motley Fool, 7 August 2026.
