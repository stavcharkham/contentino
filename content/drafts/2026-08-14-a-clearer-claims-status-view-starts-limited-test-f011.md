# A clearer claims-status view starts limited testing on September 1

Starting 1 September 2026, Lemonade is testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York.

The test begins on that date, and the group is limited to those 200 invited customers, so it's worth being precise about what's actually being tried, and what isn't changing.

WHAT CHANGED

The test only changes how status and next steps are explained in the claims view. That's it. Coverage, claim decisions, payment timing and eligibility all stay exactly as they are, for everyone, including the customers in the test group.

The team wants to learn one specific thing: whether customers can understand their next step without needing to contact support. That's a question the team is trying to answer, not a result it's promising.

IN THEIR WORDS

Maya Chen, who works on the claims product team, put it this way at the team's weekly claims product meeting on 12 August 2026:

"The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is."

WHAT THIS ISN'T

A few things worth being direct about:

This isn't available to everyone. It's a limited test with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York.

This doesn't speed up claim decisions. Nothing about how claims are decided or when payments happen is changing.

This doesn't guarantee fewer calls to support. The team is measuring whether customers understand their next step on their own. That's an open question, not a claim about the outcome.

This isn't a preview of a full launch. No decision about a wider rollout has been made, and none is implied here.

And to be clear: nothing about coverage or eligibility changes, for anyone, as part of this test.

Source: Weekly claims product meeting, 12 August 2026.
