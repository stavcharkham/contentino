# A clearer way to see claims status: now testing with 200 renters in New York

Starting 1 September, Lemonade is testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. The test changes only how status and next steps are explained—not coverage, claim decisions, payment timing, or customer eligibility.

The team will measure whether customers can understand their next step without contacting support.

**What changed:** Only the explanation of claim statuses and next steps, for the invited group.

**What this is not:** It's not available to everyone, doesn't speed up claim decisions, and doesn't guarantee fewer support contacts. No decision has been made about a full launch.

"The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is."
— Maya Chen, weekly claims product meeting, 12 August 2026
