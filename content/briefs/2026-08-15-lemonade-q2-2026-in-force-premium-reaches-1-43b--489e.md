# Brief: Lemonade Q2 2026 results

## The story

Lemonade reported second-quarter 2026 results showing growth accelerating rather than
levelling off, alongside improving unit economics. In-force premium reached $1.43 billion, up
32.5% year over year, which management described as the eleventh consecutive quarter of
accelerating growth. Revenue grew 79% to $294 million. The gross loss ratio improved to 60%
from 67%. Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed 54% to $19 million.

## Why now

The results were released on the Q2 2026 earnings call on 7 August 2026. The eleventh straight
quarter of acceleration and the halving of the adjusted EBITDA loss are the reason this is
newsworthy now, not a general product message.

## What changed

- In-force premium: $1.43 billion, up 32.5% year over year.
- Eleventh consecutive quarter of accelerating growth.
- Revenue: $294 million, up 79%.
- Gross loss ratio: 60%, improved from 67%.
- Adjusted EBITDA loss: $19 million, narrowed 54%.
- Management reiterated full-year guidance.
- Management projected positive adjusted EBITDA in Q4 2026.
