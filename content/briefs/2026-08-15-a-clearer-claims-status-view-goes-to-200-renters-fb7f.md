# A clearer claims-status view goes to 200 renters in New York

Starting 1 September, Lemonade is testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. The test changes one thing: how status and next steps are explained. It does not change coverage, claim decisions, payment timing or customer eligibility. The team will measure whether customers can understand their next step without contacting support. That's the whole question being asked here, and it's worth asking: when you're in the middle of a claim, knowing what happens next is most of what you want to know.

## Why now

The test period begins on 1 September 2026 with a defined group of 200 invited customers, so the scope and the start date are both fixed. The decision was recorded in the weekly claims product meeting on 12 August 2026.

## What changed

Only the explanation of claim status and next steps changes for the invited group. Coverage, claim decisions, payment timing and customer eligibility all stay exactly as they are.

## Quote

> The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is.
>
> — Maya Chen, in the weekly claims product meeting, 12 August 2026

Source excerpt: Maya said: "The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is."

## Not saying

- This is not available to everyone. It's a test with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York.
- It does not speed up claim decisions.
- It does not guarantee fewer support contacts. The team is measuring understanding, not promising a result.
- No decision has been made about a full launch.
