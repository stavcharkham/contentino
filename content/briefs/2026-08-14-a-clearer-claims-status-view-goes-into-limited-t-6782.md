# A clearer claims-status view goes into limited testing on 1 September

Starting 1 September 2026, Lemonade will test a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. The test only changes how status and next steps are explained. It does not change coverage, claim decisions, payment timing or customer eligibility. The team will measure whether customers understand their next step without contacting support.

## Why now

The test begins on 1 September 2026, and the group is limited to 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York, so this is the moment to be precise about what's being tried and what isn't changing.

## What changed

How status and next steps are explained in the claims view. That's it. Coverage, claim decisions, payment timing and eligibility all stay exactly as they are.

## Quote

> The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is.
>
> — Maya Chen, weekly claims product meeting, 12 August 2026

Source excerpt: Maya said: "The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is."

## Not saying

- Not saying the feature is available to everyone. It's a test with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York.
- Not saying it speeds up claim decisions. The test doesn't change claim decisions or payment timing.
- Not saying it guarantees fewer support contacts. The team is measuring whether customers understand their next step without contacting support, which is a question, not a promise.
- Not saying the test will become a full launch. No launch decision is in the source.
- Not saying anything changes about coverage or eligibility, because nothing does.
