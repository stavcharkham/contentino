# We're testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited customers

Starting 1 September 2026, we're running a four-week test of a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. The view explains an existing claim's status and what the next step is. That's the whole change: the wording, not the mechanics. Coverage, eligibility, claim decisions and payment timing all stay exactly as they are. During the test, we'll measure two things: whether invited customers can identify their next step without contacting support, and whether the clearer wording reduces repeat questions about status. A reduction is what we're aiming for, not something we're promising. The results will tell us what customers actually understand. We haven't decided whether this becomes a permanent feature.

## Why now

The test window is fixed and narrow: it opens on 1 September 2026 and closes four weeks later. Anything written about it now has to describe an invitation-only test in one state, because that's what exists. The findings don't exist yet.

## What changed

For the 200 invited customers, only the way their existing claim status and next step are explained. Nothing else about their policy or claim changes.

## Quote

> The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is.
>
> — Maya Chen, Claims Product Lead

## Not saying

- The feature is available to everyone. It's invitation-only, limited to 200 renters-insurance customers in New York.
- The test changes coverage, eligibility, claim decisions or payment timing. It doesn't.
- The test speeds up claim decisions or payments. It doesn't affect either.
- The test guarantees fewer support contacts. Reducing repeat status questions is a goal we're measuring, not an outcome we're promising.
- The test will become a permanent feature or a full launch. That decision hasn't been made.
- This is a "new claims experience." That description is broader than what's changing. It's a simpler claims-status view.
