# A four-week test of clearer claims-status wording starts 1 September

Starting 1 September, we're running a four-week invitation-only test of clearer claims-status wording with 200 New York customers. The test changes explanations only. It doesn't change coverage, claim decisions, payments or eligibility, and the wording a customer sees during the test carries no different meaning for their claim than the wording it replaces. What we're measuring is narrow and specific: whether people can tell what the next step is without needing to contact support. This is a test, not a launch, and we're not saying yet whether the wording will roll out more widely.

## Why now

The test has a fixed start date of 1 September and runs four weeks, so the window and the participant group are already set: 200 invited customers in New York, and nobody else.

## What changed

Claims-status explanations were rewritten for clarity. Coverage, decisions, payments and eligibility are unchanged.

## Quote

> The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is.
>
> — Maya, product meeting

## Not saying

- This isn't a launch or a general availability announcement.
- We're not promising an outcome, a result or a wider rollout.
- No coverage, claim decision, payment or eligibility rule changes for anyone, inside the test or outside it.
- We're not saying claims will move faster or be easier to resolve.
- Availability isn't open: participation is invitation-only, limited to 200 New York customers.
- We're not reporting results, because the test hasn't run yet.
