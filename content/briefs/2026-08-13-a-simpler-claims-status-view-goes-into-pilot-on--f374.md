# A simpler claims status view goes into pilot on 1 September

Lemonade is piloting a simpler way to see where a claim stands. Starting 1 September, 200 invited customers in New York will get the new claims status view. The question the pilot is built to answer is a narrow one: can someone read the screen and understand their next step without contacting support? That's the measure. Not satisfaction scores, not speed, just whether the status view explains itself. The pilot is limited to invited customers in New York, and it does not change how claims are decided or what a policy covers. Results from the pilot will determine what happens next, and a wider release isn't guaranteed.

## Why now

The pilot has a fixed start date of 1 September and a defined group of 200 invited customers in New York, so the scope and timing are now settled enough to describe accurately.

## What changed

Maya confirmed in the weekly product meeting that the simpler claims status view is moving into a pilot: 200 invited customers in New York, beginning 1 September, measured on whether people understand the next step without contacting support.
