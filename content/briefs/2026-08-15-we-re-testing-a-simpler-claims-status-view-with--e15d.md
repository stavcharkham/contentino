# We're testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited customers in New York

Starting 1 September 2026, we're running a four-week test of a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. The view explains an existing claim's status and next steps in clearer language. It doesn't change coverage, eligibility, claim decisions or payment timing. Over the four weeks, the team will measure two things: whether invited customers can identify their next step without contacting support, and whether the new wording reduces repeat questions about status. A reduction is a goal, not a promised outcome. The test is invitation-only, not a general launch, and we haven't decided whether it will become a permanent feature. The results will tell us what customers actually understand.

## Why now

The test begins on 1 September 2026 and runs for four weeks, so the window for describing it accurately is before results exist. Anything written outside the team now needs to say what's being tested, with whom, and what isn't changing.

## What changed

Only the way an existing claim's status and next step are explained, for 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. Coverage, eligibility, claim decisions and payment timing are unchanged.

## Quote

> The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is.
>
> — Maya Chen, Claims Product Lead, Lemonade
