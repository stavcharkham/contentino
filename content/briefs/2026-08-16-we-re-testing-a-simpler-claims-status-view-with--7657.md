# We're testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 renters in New York

Starting 1 September 2026, we're inviting 200 renters-insurance customers in New York to try a simpler claims-status view. The test runs for four weeks.

Here's what we want to learn. First, whether people can look at the screen and identify their next step without asking anyone. Second, whether the new wording reduces the number of repeat questions customers bring to support.

That's the whole scope. This is a change to how claim status is presented, not a change to how claims are reviewed or decided. Taking part in the test doesn't affect the outcome of a claim, the timing of a decision, or the timing of a payment. Those are handled the same way for everyone.

We'll know more when the four weeks are up.

## Why now

The weekly claims product meeting on 12 August 2026 set the test parameters: 200 invited customers, New York renters insurance, a 1 September 2026 start, four weeks. Publishing before the invitations go out gives customers and support teams the same description of what the test is and what it isn't.

## What changed

A test of a simpler claims-status view moved from discussion to a dated plan with a defined audience, duration and two measures. Nothing about claims handling changed.

## Purpose

- Serves: Comms and the claims product team, plus support leads who will field questions from invited customers during the test window.
- Job: Give one sourced, on-record description of the test that internal teams and customers can repeat without adding speed, outcome or support-volume claims the meeting didn't support.
- Metric: The two test measures themselves: whether invited customers can identify their next step from the view, and whether the new wording reduces repeat support questions. For the post specifically, no support escalations or corrections about scope, outcomes or payment timing during the four-week window.
- Shelf life: Goes stale on 29 September 2026, when the four-week test ends. Refresh triggers: results are available, the start date or four-week duration moves, the audience changes from 200 invited New York renters-insurance customers, or the test is cancelled or extended.

## Not saying

- Not calling it a new claims experience. The approved description is a simpler claims-status view.
- Not saying it speeds up decisions or payments. Neither was measured or claimed in the meeting.
- Not implying the view affects claim outcomes.
- Not promising fewer support contacts. Reducing repeat questions is what we're measuring, not a result we can announce.
- Not describing results. The test hasn't started.
- Not extending the test beyond invited New York renters-insurance customers, or implying a wider rollout.
