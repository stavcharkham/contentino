# A clearer claims-status view goes into testing with 200 New York renters customers

Starting 1 September 2026, Lemonade is testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. The test changes one thing: how status and next steps are explained. It does not change coverage, claim decisions, payment timing or customer eligibility. The team will measure whether customers understand their next step without contacting support.

## Why now

The test start date is set for 1 September 2026, and the scope was confirmed in the weekly claims product meeting on 12 August 2026. Anything said about it before results exist should stay inside what the meeting actually approved: an invited group of 200 renters customers in New York, and an explanation change only.

## What changed

Only the explanation of claims status and next steps changes for the invited test group. Coverage, claim decisions, payment timing and eligibility are unchanged.

## Quote

> The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is.
>
> — Maya Chen, weekly claims product meeting, 12 August 2026

Source excerpt: Maya said: "The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is."

## Not saying

- We're not saying the feature is available to everyone. It's limited to 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York.
- We're not saying it speeds up claim decisions. The test only changes how status and next steps are explained.
- We're not saying it guarantees fewer support contacts. The team is measuring understanding, and results don't exist yet.
- We're not saying this test will become a full launch. No launch decision has been made.
- We're not saying anything changes about coverage, payment timing or eligibility, because it doesn't.
