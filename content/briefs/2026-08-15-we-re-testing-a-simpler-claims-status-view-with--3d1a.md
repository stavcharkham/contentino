# We're testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited customers

Starting 1 September 2026, we're testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. The test runs for four weeks. What changes is the way an existing claim's status and next step are explained. Coverage, eligibility, claim decisions and payment timing don't change for anyone in the test. We'll measure two things: whether invited customers can identify their next step without contacting support, and whether the new wording reduces repeat questions about status. A reduction is a goal, not a promised outcome. This is an invitation-only test, not a general launch, and we haven't decided whether the view will become a permanent feature.

## Why now

The test begins 1 September 2026 with a fixed four-week window and a defined group of 200 invited customers, so the scope and dates are known before results exist. Writing about it now means describing what's being tested, not what it proves.

## What changed

Only the wording that explains an existing claim's status and next step, for the 200 invited customers in the test. Nothing about coverage, eligibility, claim decisions or payment timing changes.

## Quote

> The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is.
>
> — Maya Chen, Claims Product Lead, Lemonade
