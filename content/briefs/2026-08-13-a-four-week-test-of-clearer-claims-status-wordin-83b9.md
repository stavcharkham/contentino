# A four-week test of clearer claims-status wording starts 1 September

Starting 1 September, 200 invited New York customers will see clearer wording on their claims-status screens. It's a four-week, invitation-only test, and it changes explanations only. Coverage, claim decisions, payments and eligibility all stay exactly as they are. What the team is measuring is understanding: whether people who read the new wording know what their next step is. That's the whole scope, and it isn't a launch.

## Why now

The test window is fixed and short: it opens on 1 September and runs four weeks, so the 200 invited New York customers are the only audience during that period.

## What changed

The wording that explains claims status changes for this test group. Nothing about coverage, decisions, payments or eligibility changes, and no outcome is being promised.
