# Lemonade Q2 2026 results

## The story
In-force premium reached $1.43 billion, up 32.5% year over year. That is the eleventh
consecutive quarter in which the growth rate accelerated. Revenue grew 79% to $294 million.
The gross loss ratio improved to 60% from 67%. Adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed 54% to $19
million.

## Why now
The Q2 2026 earnings call took place on 7 August 2026. Management reiterated full-year
guidance and projected positive adjusted EBITDA in Q4 2026, which makes this the quarter
where the path to that milestone becomes specific rather than directional.

## What changed
Growth and loss ratio moved in the same direction at the same time: the book got bigger
while claims cost less per premium dollar. The narrowing EBITDA loss is the arithmetic of
those two together, not a cost-cutting story.
