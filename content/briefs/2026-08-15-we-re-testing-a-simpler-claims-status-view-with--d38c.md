# We're testing a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited customers

Starting 1 September 2026, we're running a four-week test of a simpler claims-status view with 200 invited renters-insurance customers in New York. The view explains an existing claim's status and next steps. It doesn't change coverage, eligibility, claim decisions or payment timing. During the test, we'll measure two things: whether invited customers can identify their next step without contacting support, and whether the new wording reduces repeat questions about status. A reduction is a goal, not a promised outcome. This is an invitation-only test, and we haven't decided whether it will become a permanent feature.

## Why now

The test starts on 1 September 2026 and runs for four weeks, so the window for writing about it as work in progress is now. The results don't exist yet, and the story is the test itself: clearer explanations of status and next steps for a small invited group.

## What changed

For the 200 invited customers, only the way the existing claim status and next step are explained changes. Nothing else moves.

## Quote

> The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is.
>
> — Maya Chen, Claims Product Lead

Source excerpt: Quote attributable to Maya Chen: "The goal is to make the next step clear, not to make the claim look simpler than it is."

## Not saying

- The feature is available to everyone. It's invitation-only, limited to 200 renters-insurance customers in New York.
- The test changes coverage, eligibility, claim decisions or payment timing. It does not.
- The test speeds up claim decisions or payments.
- The test guarantees fewer support contacts. Reducing repeat status questions is a goal we're measuring, not a promise.
- The test will become a permanent feature or a full launch. That decision hasn't been made.
- This is not a "new claims experience." Call it a simpler claims-status view, because that's what's changing.
